## 🆕 What's new in v2.9.7

### 📦 Batch Queue finishes the job
• **Stuck error windows are closed:** when Roblox shows "Installer encountered a critical error" (or a similar error box) during a queue, the queue now closes just that broken window. Before, it kept the queue waiting forever.
• **Failed accounts are retried:** accounts that didn't open because of the installer, a network error or a window that never appeared are put at the **end of the queue** and tried again (up to 2 more times; change it in **Retries** next to Accounts per batch).
• **Security check accounts are set aside:** they aren't retried (they would just hit the check again). At the end they're selected for you; log in to them once, solve the check, then join again.
• **No more racing through batches:** if a whole batch fails right away, the queue waits 30 seconds (Roblox trouble) or **pauses** with the reason (anything else) instead of skipping every batch with nothing opening. Press **Resume** after fixing it.
• **Next Batch:** one press opens exactly one batch, and the status shows "opening batch 4…".
• **Summary at the end:** e.g. "18 opened · 1 of them after a retry · 1 needs a security check". The leftovers are selected so you can join them again.
• No error popup after every batch while a queue runs; everything is in the Console and the summary.

### 🧪 Account Creator
• **Stop button:** a small window shows progress while accounts are created. **Stop** starts no new accounts; everything already created is kept. Close a signup browser that is still open to end it immediately.
• **New results window:** counts at the top, the full list of created accounts with **Copy all usernames**, and every failure with its reason.

### 💾 Automatic account backups
Settings → Misc → **Auto Backup**
• **Back up accounts automatically** (on by default): a protected backup is saved when the app starts and after accounts are added or removed (at most once an hour). The newest 10 are kept.
• **Backup now**, **Restore from backup** (pick by date and account count, or browse for any .rambak file) and **Open backups folder**.
• **Backup password…** (recommended): with it, backups can be restored on **any PC**. Without it they only open on this PC.
• **Extra backup folder…**: also save each backup to a USB drive or a Google Drive / OneDrive folder.
