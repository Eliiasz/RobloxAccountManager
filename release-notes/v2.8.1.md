## What's new in 2.8.1

- **Updates restart cleanly**: after updating, the app reopens by itself without the "Failed to load Python DLL" error
- **Update popup**: "Skip This Version" removed. Press **Later** to close it; it shows again next time until you update
- **Discord server** button moved to the top right of the Actions panel, with the new invite link

If the app showed "Failed to load Python DLL" while updating to this version, just open it again. That was the previous version's updater, and it's fixed from here on.
