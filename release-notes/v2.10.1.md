## 🛠️ What's new in v2.10.1

### 🛡️ Account protection badge moved to the top
• The encryption badge moved from beside "Account List" into the **top bar**, between the app name and the window buttons.
• It now shows as a small rounded badge with a shield icon, in plain words instead of capital letters in brackets:
  • **Device Locked** (green), formerly "HARDWARE ENCRYPTED": your saved accounts are encrypted with a key tied to this PC and only open on this computer.
  • **Password Locked** (blue), formerly "PASSWORD ENCRYPTED": encrypted with your password, so they open on any PC but only with that password.
  • **Unprotected** (red), formerly "NOT ENCRYPTED": stored without encryption.
• Hover over the badge for that explanation. **Click it** to jump straight to Settings → Misc → Encryption.

### 📊 Full RAM / CPU line
• With the badge gone, the line next to "Account List" has room for the whole thing again, e.g. **"1 running · RAM 10.7 / 16 GB (67%) · CPU 23%"**. Before, it was cut off.

### ⚡ Updating
• Coming from v2.10.0, the app downloads a **small patch** instead of the whole exe. It installs automatically.
