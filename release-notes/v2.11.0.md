**v2.11.0**
- Cleaner look: rounded glowing cards, sharper text, smoother hover/press animations and fades
- New themes: Mono Glass (default for new users), Violet Glow and Sky Paper (light)
- Settings → Themes is now a theme gallery with "Restart now"; custom colors and backgrounds removed
- "N running" only counts real Roblox game windows; other Roblox processes are logged in the Console
- Cookies are only checked from Account Health ("Check Cookies on Startup" is off)
- Batch Queue never pauses by itself; Kill All moves on to the next batch
- Automatic backups every 24 hours, newest 3 kept; manual backups are never deleted
- Backups use your last Export Accounts password
- Keeps 10 log files by default; the oldest is deleted when a new one is made
- Low RAM Limit defaults to 300 MB
- No more "What's new" popup after updating
