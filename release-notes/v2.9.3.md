## 🆕 What's new in v2.9.3

### 🔒 Lock accounts
Keep important accounts running when you press Kill All.
• Click ⋯ on an account (or right-click) → **Lock**. Works on several selected accounts at once.
• Locked accounts show a 🔒 next to the ⋯ button.
• **Kill All Roblox** (button or shortcut key) closes every Roblox window **except** locked accounts.
• Batch Queue ignores locked windows, so it still moves on to the next batch.
• Click ⋯ → **Unlock** to remove it. Locks are saved after restart.

### 👥 Join User box
Join a friend or any player's current server without a Place ID.
• Type their username in the **Join User** box (under Join Place ID).
• Select your accounts and press the join icon next to the box.
• Works without being friends if the target's Roblox privacy "Who can join me?" is set to **Everyone**. Otherwise your accounts must be their friends.
• The box remembers your recent targets.
• You'll get a clear message if they're offline, not in a game, or their privacy blocks it.

### 🎨 Layout
• **Refresh List** and **Roblox Home** now sit side by side, like Hotkey and Performance.
